    Demo rules are built from DAP published material: the Alex Plus FAQ on dap.com (including its shower answer and 30-minute paint time), DAP's own retailer replies, and each product line's stated use. A production finder would read these rules straight from PIM product data, which is exactly the job this role owns.

    Why a finder?

    DAP's old Caulk + Sealant Selector still lives on a legacy site. GE Sealants puts its finder on its homepage. This moves the tool back where shoppers start.

    Why the warning?

    The top 1-star theme is the wrong product in the wrong place, like latex caulk in a shower. The finder stops that before the buy, not in a reply after the fail.

    What it feeds

    Every answer is product-discovery data: which projects people start, where they get stuck, and which warnings they hit. That is roadmap input for the Website Manager.
